# Duckpad 0.7.2

Keep the new-version badge visible after dismissing the update window, cancelling installation, or encountering an update error. Click the badge again to reopen the update window. Explicitly skipping a version still clears the badge, as does a subsequent check that finds no available update.

Move the badge up by 4 points to align it with the window controls. The status bar and editor layout are unchanged.

App and helpers: **0.7.2 (46)**. Requires **macOS 13 or later**. Distribution includes Apple Silicon and Intel binaries.

## Installation

Download `Duckpad-0.7.2-universal.dmg`, open it, and drag Duckpad into Applications. The ZIP alternative is `Duckpad-0.7.2-universal.zip`. Verify downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Users on 0.7.0 or later can update in the app once the signed Sparkle feed is published; older versions require manual installation.

The app is ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or Apple notarized. Sparkle archives use a separate Ed25519 signature.

## Validation and limitations

The dismissal and badge-alignment regressions failed before the fixes and passed afterward. All 15 targeted updater and app-information tests passed, including explicit skip, cancellation, update errors, no-update results, and repeated presentation. A separate app with a local update feed verified dismissal, reopening from the badge, explicit skip, and alignment with the window controls.

This release does not change text input handling or claim to fix the intermittent Korean/English Caps Lock input issue. Physical Intel/macOS 13 execution and Apple notarization were not performed. Final universal package and update-feed verification results are recorded in the published release.
