# Duckpad 0.7.1

Fix keyboard focus after switching tabs with Option-Command-Left/Right or the last-used-tab command. Typing now continues in the destination editor without clicking the document first. Focus transfers as the editor changes, without waiting for session persistence. The fix also covers editor groups and preserves focus in other controls when they were already active.

App and helpers: **0.7.1 (45)**. Requires **macOS 13 or later**. Distribution includes Apple Silicon and Intel binaries.

## Installation

Download `Duckpad-0.7.1-universal.dmg`, open it, and drag Duckpad into Applications. The ZIP alternative is `Duckpad-0.7.1-universal.zip`. Verify downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Users on 0.7.0 can update in the app once the signed Sparkle feed is published; older versions require manual installation.

The app is ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or Apple notarized. Sparkle archives use a separate Ed25519 signature.

## Validation and limitations

The keyboard-navigation regression failed before the fix and passed afterward, including focus at activation and typing after activation persistence in previous, next, and last-used tabs. Focus preservation checks cover single and grouped editors and verify that other controls retain focus. Existing editor-group and Korean composition/Undo/Redo checks are included in the targeted regression run. The existing edit gate during a pending tab-activation transaction is unchanged.

The reported intermittent duplicate character when switching Korean/English with Caps Lock was not reproduced, including a manual test. This release does not change IME handling or claim to fix that issue.

Physical Intel/macOS 13 execution and Apple notarization were not performed. Final package and update-feed verification results are recorded in the published release.
